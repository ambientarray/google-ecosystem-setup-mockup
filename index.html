Common questions

What counts as one tag?

A single tag firing on one trigger using a standard template, for an event your site already sends to the dataLayer. Complex custom events that need new site code are quoted separately so you always know the scope.

Do you edit my live site directly?

No. We build in a separate GTM workspace and version. You approve the specific changes, then we publish. We export your current version first so there is always a rollback.

What do you need from me?

Edit access to your GTM container and GA4 property, added to our manager account, plus a working cookie consent banner if you collect personal data. That is it.

When does the clock start?

The 2-business-day window starts once access is confirmed and your intake is complete, not at payment.
